Meet Chumisa Haya

Four years ago I started baking sourdough on my kitchen counter because I missed the bread my grandmother used to make. Today, Haya's Artisan Bakery feeds our neighbourhood with loaves that take 36 hours from starter to slice – and every hour is worth it.

My mission: to bring honest, handcrafted bread back to your table. No shortcuts. No additives. Just flour, water, salt, and time.

Our Values

  • Quality first: We source local stoneground flour.
  • Community: We supply coffee shops and donate leftovers to shelters.
  • Tradition meets creativity: Classic techniques, proudly South African flavours.
